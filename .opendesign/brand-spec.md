# Moscow Cigar — Brand Spec

## Color System
- **Background**: `#FAF8F5` — warm cream, luxury paper feel
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — clean white for cards
- **Foreground**: `#2C2416` — rich dark brown, near-black
- **Muted**: `#8A7E6E` — warm grey-brown for secondary text
- **Border**: `#E8E0D6` — subtle warm divider
- **Accent (Primary)**: `#7A1F2E` — burgundy, signature luxury red
- **Accent (Secondary)**: `#B87A3A` — cognac gold for highlights
- **Accent (Tertiary)**: `#F5EFE8` — warm cream tint for subtle fills

## Typography
- **Display**: `Playfair Display` — high-contrast serif for headlines, numbers
- **Body**: System sans-serif stack — clean, readable
- **Mono**: `SF Mono` — for prices and data

## Posture
- Serif numerals with tabular-nums for prices
- Hairline borders, no heavy shadows
- 4px border radius (subtle, not bubbly)
- Generous whitespace, editorial feel
- One accent color (burgundy) used sparingly
